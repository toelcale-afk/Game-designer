The 4-direction button draws one 2x2 sheet (front, back / left, right), cuts it into four matching sprites and loads them as the directions. It replaces any back, left or right already made. Save 2x2 turnaround stores the four views as one PNG in that same order.

Pick Back, Left or Right, then tap Generate: the first one is drawn by Gemini from your front sprite (1 image). Left and right mirror each other for free once one exists. Then pose that direction with the AI buttons below, and repeat for each direction. The export includes every direction you have made.

Each AI button redraws your character in 4 poses (using the sprite as reference) and uses them as that animation's row. Each costs 4 to 8 image generations, since a pose that adds or loses a lot of body is retried once. Shoot uses the character's own ranged weapon, or a sci-fi rifle if it has none.


This redraws only the selected pose (1 to 2 images), using your note and the current pose as guidance, so you don't have to rerun the whole set.

Skeleton (movement rig)

Pick a step, then touch the sprite: Hip sets where the body bends, Legs sets where the legs split, Arm lets you lasso the weapon arm, then Shoulder sets its pivot. The arm then swings on its own in attack, shoot, block and walking.

Load a sprite to build idle, attack and block animations.

Built-in motion squashes, leans, lunges and flashes your single sprite, so limbs don't move independently. The sheet has one row per animation: idle, move, attack, block, shoot. The body bends at the hips with the feet planted, and the legs step during Move.
